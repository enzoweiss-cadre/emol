Problem: The hybrid ICP pipeline asks LLMs (Perplexity Sonar) to find structured facts cold — company size, public status, patent counts, publication history. The fill rate report showed this fails 85%+ of the time. The model scores from absence of evidence, creating a systematic bias against companies it simply can't find.

Solution: Run a pre-enrichment pass before the LLM agents. Pull deterministic data from authoritative public sources, write it to Supabase, and inject pre-populated facts into every agent prompt. The agents confirm and interpret — they no longer need to discover.

Target Verticals & ICP Definition

Who we are enriching data for
🧬 Series A+ Biotech Primary
  • Active small molecule drug discovery pipeline
  • 10–150 employees, Series A through Series C
  • In-house medicinal chemistry team (3+ chemists)
  • Preclinical through Phase II stage
  • Examples: Series A oncology biotech, CNS startups
Why high value: Actively buying building blocks, high session volume per researcher, strong need for eMolecules services
🏭 Mid-Tier Pharma Primary
  • 500–5,000 employees
  • Established drug discovery organization
  • Multiple active programs across therapeutic areas
  • Mix of small molecule and biologic pipelines
  • Examples: regional pharma, specialty pharma companies
Why high value: Large compound orders, procurement through defined channels, multi-year relationships
🤖 AI / Computational Drug Discovery Primary
  • AI-first drug discovery platforms
  • Strong CADD / virtual screening capabilities
  • Need for make-on-demand virtual libraries
  • Often Series A–B, 20–100 employees
  • Examples: Recursion, Insilico, Exscientia-type companies
Why high value: Virtual Libraries product fit is exact match; high volume, high frequency orders
🔬 CRO / CDMO (with discovery services) Secondary
  • Contract research orgs with medicinal chemistry capacity
  • Discovery chemistry and SAR services
  • NOT clinical-only CROs (auto-disqualified)
  • Active synthesis and HTS capabilities
  • Examples: Evotec (chemistry division), WuXi AppTec discovery
Why secondary: Buy in bulk but price-sensitive; good for Screening Compounds and Building Blocks
Automatic Disqualifiers — these verticals are excluded regardless of other signals:
Pure biologics (antibody / gene therapy / cell therapy / mRNA only)
Medical device companies
Diagnostics-only companies
Manufacturing-only (no discovery division)
Clinical-trials-only CROs
Academic institutions without industry partnerships
